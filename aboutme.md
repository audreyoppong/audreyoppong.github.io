## Experience
  
### Work
As a Student Assistant for Tech's BridgeUP STEM program, I facilitate programming and computational thinking education for high school students. My role is to assist with classroom instruction, troubleshoot coding exercises, and coordinate technical workshops such that lessons are more accessible to underrepresented students.

### Research
At Tech, I work in the Cognitive Motor Control Lab under Dr. Lewis Wheaton and investigate human upper-limb motor control, tool use, and movement dynamics through Serial Reaction-Time Tasks. My role is using EEGLAB and The MotionMonitor to process neural and kinematic datasets with intentions of identifying biomarkers of motor learning. In the Summer of 2027, I interned at the Rehabilitation Biomechanics Lab at the University of Michigan and contributed to research on regenerative peripheral nerve interfaces. Using MATLAB, I quantified and mapped sensation patterns to advance assistive device design and sensory restoration protocols.

### Clubs & Activities
Extracurricularly, I am engaged in campus STEM outreach through Synapse @ GT, a brain-computer interfacing (BCI) organization. Through Synapse, I've gained experience decoding neural activity; I am also on the newsletter team and participate in biweekly journal club discussions. I'm also part of the Accessible Prosthetics Initiative's Education Committee, where I'm leading production of an interactive storybook for pediatric amputees and their caregivers to familiarize themselves with prosthetics care practices and appropriate terminology to use. Lastly, I serve on the Student Government Association's Student Needs Committee, which provides several forms of support to the student community. During meetings, I've influenced decisions on disbursement of the Undergraduate SGA Emergency Fund, matched students with hosts for our annual Home for the Holidays program, and supported regular fundraising efforts.

Previously, I served on the Youth Advisory Board for the National Girls Collaborative Project (NGCP) and as a NSBE Jr. Ambassador; through both opportunities I advocated for more inclusive STEM pipelines for young women and Black students in technical fields. I also volunteered with KID Museum, a local interactive STEM makerspace, to guide youth through maker-space builds, STEAM lessons, and electronics prototyping.
