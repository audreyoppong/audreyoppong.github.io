## Learn 
### About the Brain and Body
I want to understand how the nervous system plans movement and processes sensory feedback. As a Computer Engineering major, I plan to pair signal processing with neuroscience. My ECE coursework in classes like HW/SW Systems Programming and Signals and Systems, combined with hands-on research in cognitive motor control, provides a foundation in the operation of biological signals in real time. 

## Practice
### Neural Interfacing, Circuit Design, Machine Learning
My research interest lies in the decoding layer of BCIs. I want to gain experience building low-latency systems that translate neural firing patterns into real-time kinematic commands. Ideally, research in EEG signal analysis, embedded circuit design, and machine learning will help me build bidirectional interfaces. I'll seek opportunities to apply these tools through lab work, research internships, and personal projects.

## Share
### Science Communication
I want to ensure that all of the work I do is approachable for audiences at all levels of expertise so the individuals impacted by emerging tech will understand and shape it. So far, I've used my backgrounds in narrative and technical writing while on the Synapse @ GT newsletter, developing educational materials for API, and giving poster presentations. These experiences have built, and will continue to build, my comfort translating hard science into clear dialogue. 

## Educate
### Be a Teacher
One of my long-term goals is to expand access in STEM through teaching and mentorship. Supporting high school programmers in BridgeUP and mentoring younger students through NSBE Jr. and the NGCP has shown me how confidence grows when learning is enabled. I intend to continue these efforts through college by volunteering with local STEM enrichment programs and potentially working as a Teaching Assistant. Ultimately, I hope to become a professor and principal investigator so I may lead a neural engineering lab and help scholars from all levels and backgrounds realize their potential.
